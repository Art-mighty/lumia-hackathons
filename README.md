## What it is

An isometric online RPG for primary-school kids. The maths is part of the
world: in Hall of Scales you balance numbered stones until both pans weigh the
same. English and Russian, voiced, web and Android.

## Personalisation

Every round of every trial is recorded per child against 192 Grade 1–4
topics. Lumia is designed so AI uses that profile to adjust the next trial
on the child's weak skill only. See [PERSONALISATION.md](PERSONALISATION.md).
